A Word from our Founder

"Poetry is the shortest distance
between two hearts."

Poetry Garden was never meant to be an 'industry'. It was meant to be an invitation. I remember sitting in my balcony during a rainy Mumbai evening, wishing I had someone to share the weight of a poem with.

This community is my response to that wish. We prioritize the 'slow interaction'—the kind that leaves you feeling more human, less processed.

Manu Vaishali

Founder & Curator

The Soul of Mumbai's Spoken Word

We believe poetry is not just on the page, but in the collective breath of a room. Our mission is to preserve the slow beauty of conversation.
