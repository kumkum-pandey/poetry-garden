Elara Vance

Sufi Poet & Contemporary Calligrapher

ink_pen

The Voice Behind the Verse

Born amidst the bustling alleys of old Damascus and now residing in the quiet hills of Tuscany, Elara's work is a bridge between worlds. Her poetry does not merely speak; it breathes the scent of ancient dust and fresh rain.

Drawing heavily from classical Sufi traditions, she weaves modern existential yearning into structured, rhyming forms. Her calligraphy, often accompanying her spoken word performances, acts as a visual anchor to the fleeting nature of sound.

"We are but ink on water,
Trying to write our names
Before the ripple fades."
