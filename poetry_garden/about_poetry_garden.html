The Story of Poetry Garden

"We didn't just build a stage; we cultivated a soil where whispers could become roars, and fragile verses could find strength in community."

Founded in the quiet corners of Mumbai's cultural heart, Poetry Garden began as a small gathering of three dreamers with dog-eared journals. It was born from a simple realization: in a world moving too fast, we needed a place to slow down and listen.

Today, it has evolved into a sanctuary for storytellers, poets, and thinkers—a living manuscript where every evening adds a new chapter to our collective memory.

Our Purpose

The Mission

To preserve the sanctity of the written and spoken word by providing an elevated, intimate platform for authentic expression. We aim to bridge the gap between traditional literary heritage and modern storytelling through curated experiences that provoke thought and evoke emotion.

The Vision

To be the global hearth where the world's diverse poetic souls find home.

A Word from our Founder

"Poetry is the shortest distance between two hearts."

Poetry Garden was never meant to be an 'industry'. It was meant to be an invitation. I remember sitting in my balcony during a rainy Mumbai evening, reading a poem that changed me, and wishing I had someone to share that weight with.

This community is my response to that wish. We prioritize the 'slow interaction'—the kind that leaves you feeling more human, less processed. Thank you for being a verse in this ongoing poem.

Manu Vaishali

Founder & Curator
